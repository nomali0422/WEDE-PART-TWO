*1.1 User-Centred Design 

*1.1 What is User-Centred Design* 

 

User-Centred Design means designing a product around the actual people who will use it. The focus is on understanding their problems and goals before building anything.  

 

This approach is very important for GreenLeaf because most of their problems involve users. Gardeners get lost, miss special requests, and there is no proof when a job is done. Office staff are stuck with spreadsheets and WhatsApp. If we don’t talk to gardeners, office staff, managers and customers first, the app won’t fix anything. 

 

UCD has 4 steps: learn about the users, list what they need, design the app, and test it with them. For GreenLeaf we need to ask gardeners how they work in the field, watch how office staff schedule jobs, and find out what customers want. 

 

The result will be an app that is easy to use outside, works offline, shows clear addresses, and lets gardeners upload photos. This will make the app more useful and help GreenLeaf improve communication and service.
